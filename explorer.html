What the replay assumes

Three inbound ships each feed four lots into one outbound service. The original readiness cutoff is minute 240 and departure is minute 270. Holding moves both times by the same amount.

At the cutoff, the policy checks which manifested lots have reported readiness. It waits for the remaining lots or the cap, whichever comes first. Loads ready at the cutoff qualify. Readiness is the model’s proxy for making the connection. Capacity, crane scheduling, downstream service and rebooking are outside the replay.
